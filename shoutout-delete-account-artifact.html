Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your ShoutOut account and the data associated with it. This applies to both Creator and Business accounts.

  1. Send an email to privacy@shout-out.me from the email address registered on your ShoutOut account, with the subject line Delete my account.
  2. Include your account's registered email address and whether you are a Creator or Business account, so we can locate it quickly.
  3. We will verify the request and confirm back to you by email, then process the deletion. This is completed within 30 days of a verified request.
Before you request deletion Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. Any active campaigns, pending applications, unread messages, or wallet balance tied to the account will no longer be accessible to you.

What gets deleted

Your profile information (name, bio, photos, portfolio/rate card, niches, industry details), your account credentials, connected social account tokens, messages you've sent, and reviews/reports tied to your identity are deleted or de-identified from active systems.

Partial data requests

If you'd like us to correct, export, or delete specific pieces of your data without closing your account entirely, email privacy@shout-out.me with the details — you don't need to delete your whole account to exercise this.
