Delete your ShoutOut account

You can ask us to delete your ShoutOut account and the personal data associated with it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email meeranmehdisyed@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my ShoutOut account".
  2. Send it from the email address registered to your account (or include that address and your username).
  3. We'll confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account login details and profile (name, photo, bio, handles, location, follower counts, rates, portfolio).
  • Linked social account connections and their stored access tokens.
  • Your campaign listings, applications, and chat messages.

What we may keep

  • Payment, wallet, payout and invoice records, kept as long as required by financial and tax law.
  • Reviews you left for others, which may stay visible in anonymized form.
  • Records needed to resolve open disputes, reports, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

Please withdraw or settle any wallet balance before requesting deletion. See our Privacy Policy for more.
