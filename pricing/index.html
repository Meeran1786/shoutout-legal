How payment works

  1. The business tops up its ShoutOut Wallet through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets).
  2. The business and creator agree a price for the promotion in the app.
  3. When the business approves the creator's delivered work, the agreed amount moves from the business's wallet to the creator's wallet.
  4. The creator withdraws earnings to their registered bank account.

A tax invoice or receipt is available for every top-up and payment in the app under Wallet.
